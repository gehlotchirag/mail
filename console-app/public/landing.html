The part everyone dreads

You will never touch an MX record

Google and Zoho hand you a table of DNS records and wish you luck. We connect to your registrar directly and write every one of them ourselves.

  • Authorize once

    Sign in to Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Porkbun or DigitalOcean from inside the console. That is the whole step.

  • MX, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, written for you

    Including domain verification. Correct the first time, so your mail does not land in spam while you debug a typo.

  • Somewhere else? Still fine.

    On a registrar we do not automate yet, you get a copy-paste list and a live checker that tells you the moment it propagates.

Before you ask

Common questions

How long does the move take?
Setup is minutes — domain, registrar, first mailbox. Copying historical mail runs in the background and depends on volume; a mailbox with tens of thousands of messages can take hours. Your team keeps working throughout, and you only switch over your MX record when you are ready.
Do we have to change our domain registrar?
No. Keep your domain exactly where it is. We only ask for permission to write DNS records on it, and you can revoke that at any time. If your registrar is not one of the four we automate, you add the records manually and we check them for you.
Is GST included in those prices?
No — prices are quoted exclusive of GST, the same way Zoho quotes them, so the comparison is fair. 18% GST is added at checkout and appears on your invoice.
What happens if we want to leave later?
You take everything. We run standard IMAP, so any mail client can connect and download the full archive without our involvement. Calendars and contacts export as .ics and .vcf. There is no proprietary format and no exit fee.
Can we keep using Outlook or Apple Mail?
Yes. IMAP, SMTP, POP3 and ActiveSync are all supported, so Outlook, Apple Mail, Thunderbird and the Gmail app all work. The web app and mobile apps are there if you want them, not a requirement.
How many domains can we host?
Up to 30, on every paid plan — we do not charge more for the cheaper tiers to host fewer domains. You pay per mailbox, not per domain.
What is included free?
Every new signup starts on Lite free for 12 months — up to 20 mailboxes, 5 GB each, across up to 30 domains, no credit card required. Plenty of room to move your whole team across and see whether it holds up before you pay anything.
